Demine Ukraine

About

A large part of Ukraine's land is considered potentially contaminated with explosive ordnance. No business or economic activity can happen there, and people cannot live or work on it. Together with the Ministry of Economy of Ukraine, Taktika created a brand for the country's humanitarian demining programme.
