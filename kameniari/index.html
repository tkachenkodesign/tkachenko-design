Kameniari

About

Kamenyari" (Franko to the Front) is a volunteer initiative on Instagram that raises funds for the needs of Ukrainian military personnel, while popularizing the works of Ivan Franko and the slogan "lupaty siu skalu" (break this rock) as a symbol of the collective struggle for victory. The name is derived from Ivan Franko's famous poem "Kamenyari" (The Rock-Breakers, 1878), which has taken on a new resonance during the full-scale war as a call to fight tirelessly.
