Alterra

About

Alterra is a network of private schools in Ukraine. Its first school opened in Kyiv in 2018, and today it teaches children in 12 locations across eight Ukrainian cities. Learning is built on democratic principles and an individual approach to each child's interests: children help choose their lessons, electives and learning formats.
